AI-Based Smart Parking Management System

Project Overview
The AI-Based Smart Parking Management System is a smart web-based application designed to make parking management easier, faster, and more efficient.

The system helps users find available parking spaces, book parking slots, register vehicles, and manage parking entry and exit. It also aims to use AI and IoT technologies to improve parking management and reduce the time spent searching for parking spaces.

Problem Statement
Finding an available parking space in crowded areas is difficult and time-consuming. Drivers often have to search for empty parking slots, which can cause traffic congestion, fuel wastage, and inconvenience.

Traditional parking systems may also have limited information about parking availability and vehicle management.

Proposed Solution
The proposed AI-Based Smart Parking Management System provides a digital platform for managing parking spaces efficiently.

The system allows users to view available parking slots, register their vehicles, book parking spaces, and manage vehicle entry and exit. AI and IoT technologies can be integrated to monitor parking availability and improve the overall parking experience.
